☕ Coffee Sales Analysis — Excel Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes coffee shop sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify sales trends, customer purchasing behavior, and product performance.

The project covers the complete workflow from raw data preparation and cleaning to analysis and dashboard development.

🎯 Business Questions

- How do sales change over time?
- Which coffee products generate the most sales?
- Which product categories perform best?
- What are the key sales trends?
- Which areas of the business should receive more attention?

🔍 Key Findings

1. Annual Sales Performance

- 2021 recorded the highest annual sales at approximately $13,766.11.
- Sales in 2019 and 2020 were relatively similar.
- The 2022 dataset covers only part of the year, so its total sales should not be directly compared with full-year results.

2. Geographic Sales Performance

- The United States generated approximately $35,638.89, accounting for 78.95% of total sales.
- Ireland contributed approximately $6,696.87, while the United Kingdom generated approximately $2,798.51.
- The United States was the largest market in the dataset.

3. Coffee Type Performance

- Excelsa recorded the highest sales among the four coffee types at approximately $12,306.44.
- Liberica and Arabica generated approximately $12,054.08 and $11,768.50, respectively.
- Robusta recorded the lowest sales at approximately $9,005.25.

4. Roast Type Performance

- Light Roast generated the highest sales at approximately $17,354.47.
- Medium Roast generated approximately $14,600.48, followed by Dark Roast at approximately $13,179.32.

5. Top Customers

- Allis Wilmore was the highest-ranked customer by total sales at approximately $317.07.
- The analysis identified the five customers with the highest total sales, providing a starting point for customer retention analysis.

💡 Business Recommendations

1. Maintain the Leading Market: Continue monitoring sales performance in the United States and investigate opportunities to maintain customer engagement.
2. Explore Growth Opportunities: Investigate ways to increase sales in Ireland and the United Kingdom, such as targeted promotions and customer research.
3. Review Product Performance: Investigate the factors contributing to Excelsa's higher sales and evaluate Robusta's performance before making product decisions.
4. Evaluate Roast Preferences: Review sales quantities and pricing by roast type to better understand the strong performance of Light Roast.
5. Strengthen Customer Retention: Explore purchase frequency and customer spending patterns to identify opportunities for targeted loyalty initiatives.

Note: These recommendations are based on sales performance. Additional data, such as product costs, profit margins, customer counts, and purchase frequency, would be needed to validate profitability and determine the effectiveness of specific strategies.
