☕ Coffee Sales Analysis — Excel Dashboard
📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes coffee sales data using Microsoft Excel to understand sales trends, geographic performance, and product preferences.
The workflow covers data review and preparation, analysis with PivotTables, and the development of an interactive Excel dashboard.
🎯 Business Questions
How did sales change over time?
Which countries generated the most sales?
Which coffee types and roast types performed best?
Which customers ranked highest by total sales?
What areas may warrant further business investigation?

🎯 Key Insights
1. Sales Over Time
2021 recorded approximately $13,766.11 in sales.
2019 and 2020 recorded relatively similar sales totals.
The 2022 dataset is partial, ending on August 19, 2022; its total is therefore not directly comparable with complete years.
2. Geographic Performance
The United States generated approximately $35,638.89, or 78.95% of total sales.
Ireland generated approximately $6,696.87.
The United Kingdom generated approximately $2,798.51.
The United States was the largest market in this dataset. The analysis does not establish why performance differs between countries.
3. Coffee Type Performance
Excelsa recorded the highest sales among the four coffee types at approximately $12,306.44.
Liberica generated approximately $12,054.08, followed by Arabica at approximately $11,768.50.
Robusta recorded approximately $9,005.25 in sales.
4. Roast Type Performance
Light Roast generated approximately $17,354.47.
Medium Roast generated approximately $14,600.48.
Dark Roast generated approximately $13,179.32.
5. Customer Performance
Allis Wilmore ranked first by recorded sales, at approximately $317.07.
The analysis also identifies the five customers with the highest total sales. This ranking can be used as a starting point for deeper customer-retention analysis; it does not by itself show purchase frequency or loyalty.
💡 Business Recommendations
Monitor the leading market. Continue tracking U.S. sales and investigate which customer segments and products contribute most to performance.
Research other markets. Explore customer preferences and sales patterns in Ireland and the United Kingdom before deciding whether targeted campaigns are appropriate.
Investigate product differences. Compare sales quantity, average selling price, and customer mix across coffee types before making stocking or promotional decisions.
Review roast preferences. Examine quantity and price by roast type to better understand the stronger sales recorded for Light Roast.
Explore customer retention. Analyze purchase frequency and repeat-customer behavior before designing loyalty initiatives.
These are recommendations for further investigation, not proven causes or guaranteed outcomes. The workbook's sales figures alone do not establish profitability or the effectiveness of a particular strategy.

⚙️ Methodology & Limitations
The analysis is based on the sales data included in the workbook.
The 2022 data is partial and ends on August 19, 2022.
Sales are not the same as profit; product costs and operating expenses are not included in the findings presented here.
Customer rankings are based on recorded sales and do not independently measure loyalty, satisfaction, or retention.
Findings describe the dataset and should not be treated as evidence of the causes behind observed differences.
